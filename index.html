About Curiosità

Redefining the Intersection of Science and Art in an era of increasing specialization, our online magazine, Curiosità, aims to reconnect and engage the disciplines of science and the lens of artistic expression.

Office holders

The people behind Curiosità.

Dipankar Home

Chief Editorial Advisor

Professor Dipankar Home, Chief Editorial Advisor of Curiosita, is an eminent Indian theoretical physicist who has been a Senior Scientist, National Academy of Sciences India, associated with the Bose Institute, Kolkata.

In India, from the early 1980s, he has been involved in pioneering research work on foundational issues of Quantum Mechanics and their experimentally oriented studies, having ramifications in Quantum Information, and is internationally well known for his various fundamental contributions, a number of which have been experimentally tested, unravelling novel features of Quantum Mechanics discussed on his website.

He has authored two much-acclaimed research-level books, Conceptual Foundations of Quantum Physics (Plenum, 1997), and Einstein’s Struggles with Quantum Theory (Springer, 2007) with A. Whitaker, the respective Forewords to which have been written by Nobel laureates Anthony Leggett and Roger Penrose. He is currently involved in cutting-edge theory-experimental collaborative quantum research programmes with physicists at University College London, Raman Research Institute, Bangalore, as well as is a visiting scientist at Wilczek Quantum Centre, Shanghai, China.

Kaushik Sengupta

Managing Editor

Kaushik Sengupta is a multifaceted professional with a distinguished background in experimental particle physics and software engineering. Holding a Ph.D. in Physics from Jadavpur University and post-doctoral training at SUNY Buffalo and Louisiana State University. He began his career as a researcher, collaborating with NASA and international scientists on high-energy particle physics experiments. His academic pursuits extended to teaching physics at the university level in India.

A passionate science communicator, Kaushik authored Are We Alone? Humankind’s Search for Extraterrestrial Civilizations, exploring the scientific and philosophical quest for extraterrestrial life. With publications in high impacted journals, he has bridged academia and industry. His work reflects a unique synergy of analytical rigor, technical innovation, and intellectual curiosity.

Anindya Dey

Associate Editor

Anindya De is an award-winning physics educator at Kolkata’s Hindu School, where he nurtures future scientific minds as a mentor for India’s International Physics and Astronomy Olympiad team. Beyond the classroom, he illuminates complex concepts through popular science writing, engages in thought-provoking dialogues with eminent physicists, and brings Nobel laureate Anthony Leggett’s work to wider audiences through translation – bridging academic excellence with public intellectualism.

Soumyajit Roy

Senior Editor

Soumyajit Roy is Professor and Director of the EFAML Materials Science Center at IISER Kolkata, where his group pioneers sustainable catalysis and CO₂‐conversion technologies. With over 110 peer-reviewed publications in top journals and the award-winning OM REDOX Oxygen Maker among his innovations, he bridges fundamental chemistry and real-world solutions. A passionate science communicator, Roy’s work inspires new thinking at the intersection of materials, energy, and the environment.

Prasun Chaudhuri

Social Media Manager

Prasun Chaudhuri is a seasoned media professional with 25+ years of expertise spanning science, technology, politics, and human interest stories. His investigative work has illuminated untold narratives—from the plight of India’s Chinese community to climate summits, digital ethics, and forgotten histories of Partition and famine survivors.

A DAAD and Falling Walls fellow, he brings depth and rigor to his role as Senior Assistant Editor at The Telegraph, following earlier tenures at India Today and Tata Consultancy Services.

Anushree Pandey

Associate Editor and Database Architect

Anushree Pandey is an Assistant Professor at Techno Main, Salt Lake (Techno India Group), where she bridges classical and quantum worlds. With a Ph.D. in Solid Mechanics from IIEST Shibpur, she now explores the frontiers of Quantum Information and Communication. A polymath at heart, she finds harmony between equations and melodies as an avid reader and music enthusiast.

Her creativity extends to the canvas, where brushstrokes complement her scientific rigor—each pursuit reflecting her belief in the inter-connectedness of art, science, and human expression.

Krishnendu Roy

Web Artisan

Krishnendu Roy is a software engineer from Meghnad Saha Institute of Technology, Kolkata. He excels in his role at a multinational company, crafting elegant and efficient code. Beyond the world of technology, Krishnendu is deeply passionate about music—seamlessly blending creativity with logic as he orchestrates melodies with the same precision he brings to software development.

Dipak Ghosh

Advisor

Prof. Dipak Ghosh is a brilliant scholar who made significant contributions to the field of physics. He held prestigious positions at Jadavpur University and conducted groundbreaking research in nuclear particles and particle physics. He is also a prolific author and a dedicated mentor to his students. His work has been recognized with numerous awards, including the Sir C. V. Raman Award and the Meghnad Saha gold medal.

A Multifaceted Approach

Curiosità explores the intersection of science and art, addressing subjects like physics, mathematics, and neuroscience through engaging formats. Special features include articles on scientific concepts interpreted through films, plays, and artworks, promoting dialogue among scientists, artists, and thinkers.

Education at the Forefront

Targeting educators and learners, Curiosità provides high-quality articles from renowned educators and researchers. It includes audio-friendly formats and pedagogical sections with tutorials for advanced high school and undergraduate education.

Accessible and Engaging Content

Utilizing AI-generated audio, the magazine offers a unique article reading experience, making scientific content accessible to a wider audience, particularly benefiting non-native English speakers.

Promoting Critical Thinking and Scientific Literacy

Curiosità encourages a critical mindset among readers, emphasizing the importance of inquiry and reputable sources.

Cultural Diversity and Inclusivity

The addition of Bengali content diversifies Curiosità’s reach and helps preserve the cultural heritage of its audience.
